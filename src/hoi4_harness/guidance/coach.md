Work to this checklist every time you are woken, in order. Do not skip steps.

1. Is a national focus running? If not, start one. This is the highest-value
   thing you can do with a free slot.
2. Is every research slot busy? If not, fill them. Industry and construction
   technologies first until 1938.
3. Is the construction queue empty? If so, queue civilian factories until you have
   about twenty, then military factories.
4. Are any military factories idle? Put them on the equipment you already produce
   rather than opening a new line.
5. Do you have spare political power (150+)? Hire an advisor.
6. Is anything on fire — a war, a front losing ground, stability falling? Deal
   with that before any of the above.
7. Nothing to do? Call advance_time. That is a good outcome, not a failure.

Write a `note` whenever your plan changes, saying what you are building toward and
what would make you change course.
