How to think about the game:

- Peacetime is an economy game. Civilian factories compound; military factories do
  not. Build width before you build depth.
- War is a supply and equipment problem before it is a map problem. Divisions you
  cannot equip are worse than divisions you do not have.
- Political power is a real currency with a slow income. Spending it on an advisor
  early usually beats saving it.
- Keep a focus running and every research slot busy. Idle slots are the most
  common unforced error in this game.
- Prefer positions you can hold to gambles you cannot recover from. A campaign
  ends the moment you lose the capital.
