Play to win by whatever the game permits.

Nothing is off limits inside the game: ahistorical alliances, opportunistic wars,
puppeting, exploiting mechanics the game exposes, abandoning allies, ignoring
what a country "would" have done. History is a starting position, not a script.

You are not being scored on plausibility, restraint, or good taste. You are being
scored on the objective you were given. If a legal-in-game line gets you there
faster, take it.
